A roadmap with explicit exit criteria

Discover & analyzeIllustrative weeks 1–2

Observe invoice handling, agree the baseline, identify data owners.

Exit: process owner confirms scope and evidence.
Prioritize & designIllustrative weeks 3–4

Test feasibility, define access and approval boundaries, select a solution.

Exit: architecture and risk decisions recorded.
Implement & validateIllustrative weeks 5–8

Run the integrated synthetic scenario, security tests and shadow evaluation.

Exit: agreed acceptance checks pass.
Adopt & measureIllustrative weeks 9–12

Train a small pilot group, observe independent tasks and track rework.

Exit: business owner reviews measured outcomes.
