# Application strategy — Belgium

This is a search and evidence-selection strategy, not a list of active vacancies. No application or outreach has been sent. Formal education, language level, work eligibility, salary needs and commuting preferences are not supplied here and remain personal screening inputs.

## Opportunity tiers

| Tier | Role criteria | Useful proof | Screen out / be cautious |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate search | Trainee/junior functional analyst, applications support with analysis/UAT, junior business/technical product analyst, supported implementation roles | Process and acceptance docs, invoice rules, role matrix, one personally explained request trace | Mandatory credentials or language requirements you do not meet; unsupported promises of independent production delivery |
| Stretch | Associate AI implementation consultant, transformation analyst with technical prototyping, associate PM or business applications analyst requesting some experience | Coherent case, prioritization/assumptions, controlled workflow, RAG/evaluation limits and completed debugging evidence | Full ownership of live Azure/Power Platform production platforms or a client programme without mentoring |
| Long-term | AI product manager, transformation lead, Head of AI/Digital Transformation, Director of Product, CTO/VP or founder responsibility | Sustained delivery, users/outcomes, product judgment, people/financial responsibility and a demonstrated domain | Treating a six-repository portfolio as equivalent to leadership experience or professional qualification |

“Immediate” means worth screening now, not guaranteed eligibility or employability. Compare actual vacancy criteria against your own confirmed background.

## Search geography and terms

Create separate searches for Bruges/Brugge and West Flanders/West-Vlaanderen, Kortrijk, Gent/Ghent, then Belgium-accessible remote/hybrid work. Choose an honest travel limit; hybrid does not imply no office attendance or Belgian employment eligibility.

Search Dutch and English terms in pairs: `junior functioneel analist / junior functional analyst`, `business applications analyst / applicatiebeheer analyse`, `AI implementatie / associate AI consultant`, `digital transformation analyst / digitale transformatie`, `technical product analyst / product operations`. Add trainee/associate/junior when appropriate. Review tasks and supervision requirements, not title alone.

Use [VDAB job search](https://www.vdab.be/vindeenjob) for location and experience filtering, [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) for role/location searches, and the employer's own career page to verify the final posting. These entry pages were checked on 5 November 2026; no individual vacancy or opening is asserted. Do not infer an employer is hiring from its technology stack.

## Tailor the evidence

- **Analysis:** send the central overview plus process/acceptance artifacts; discuss one business rule and an exception. Avoid leading with framework counts.
- **AI implementation:** send the architecture, deterministic demo route and one guardrail/evaluation result. State cloud and tenant-runtime limits immediately.
- **Product/business technology:** send the problem, prioritization and adoption assumptions; explain one trade-off and how you would measure value.
- **Engineering-heavy opportunity:** include a source trace, failed/then-fixed practice test and a clearly scoped unaided change only after personally completing it.

Use one case-study URL and one concise project entry in an initial application. Offer deeper source/release evidence for follow-up; do not attach every report or raw logs.

## A manageable search loop

Twice weekly, spend 30 minutes identifying 5–8 plausible roles, then screen 2–3 carefully. For each record: official URL, checked date, location/work arrangement, essential criteria, experience gap, chosen track, evidence to share and next action. Prioritize a small number of tailored applications over sending the same senior-sounding profile widely. Track responses as observations, not a claim that this portfolio guarantees interviews.

For a conversation with a collaborator, ask for feedback on one boundary or requirement rather than asking them to endorse all skills. Send messages yourself from your own account after reviewing the wording. No automated outreach is part of this package.

## Remaining credibility gaps

Independent coding/diagnosis, requirements facilitation, stakeholder acceptance, real adoption measurement, actual Microsoft/Azure runtime and operational support remain unverified unless you produce personal or project evidence. The [14-day plan](OWNERSHIP-PLAN.md) addresses explainability and troubleshooting first. A mentor-supported entry role can be a better match than a title implying unsupervised architecture or consulting delivery. Update the CV and LinkedIn only after checking each personal claim.
