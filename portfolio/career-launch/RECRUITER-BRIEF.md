# Philippe Godfroy — business & AI transformation

**Direction:** business/functional analysis, AI implementation and technical product analysis. Independent project work with substantial AI-assisted development; foundational programming logic and developing independent implementation proficiency.

## The case in one minute

A fictional accounting practice needs to validate invoices, find policies and keep approval responsibility clear. Six small repositories connect process and business-case assumptions to working invoice review, controlled AI tool proposals, authorized knowledge retrieval, evaluations and a Microsoft connector artifact. The case is designed for inspection: source, independent setup, actual test records, hosted CI and a public portfolio milestone are available.

**Why it is relevant:** it shows how business rules, requirements, exceptions, system boundaries, human decisions and acceptance evidence fit together. The strongest discussion is about why a proposed action needs a separate approver, how an integration failure affects the process, and what would need measuring in a pilot.

**What a reviewer can inspect:** the accounting workspace and audit, workflow tool receipts, restricted knowledge sources, positive/unsafe evaluation controls and traceable scenario assumptions. These are synthetic local demonstrations. Genuine local-Qwen tool calling has separate evidence; Azure quality and full Copilot Studio runtime are unverified.

**What this does not establish:** a client engagement, achieved savings, enterprise deployment, senior-level qualification or independent mastery of all generated code. Personal ownership exercises are prepared for the author to complete.
